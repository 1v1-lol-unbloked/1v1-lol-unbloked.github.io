About Dodge

Dodge isn't a game you ease into — it's reaction games, and it wants fast hands and faster decisions. Roughly 1M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly. It's built on HTML5, JavaScript.

How to Play Dodge

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Dodge yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Dodge
