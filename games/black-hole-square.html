About Black Hole Square

Black Hole Square is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — puzzle games, in case the name didn't give it away. Quinten built it, running on HTML5, JavaScript. Roughly 1M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly.

How to Play Black Hole Square

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Black Hole Square yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Black Hole Square

  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
