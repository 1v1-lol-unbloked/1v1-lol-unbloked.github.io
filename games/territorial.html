Territorial

Overview

About Territorial

Territorial is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — empire games, in case the name didn't give it away. It's a HTML5, JavaScript title, made by David Tschacher. It's sitting at about 10M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog.

How to Play Territorial

Territorial's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Territorial

  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.

Why Play Territorial Here

Territorial just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.

Territorial FAQ

Can I play Territorial in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for Territorial?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
