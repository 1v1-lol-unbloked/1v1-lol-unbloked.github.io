About Getting Over It

Griffpatch built it, running on Scratch. Somewhere around 10M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. Getting Over It is a physics-based strategy games pick for anyone who'd rather plan two steps ahead than button-mash.

How to Play Getting Over It

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Getting Over It yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Getting Over It

  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
