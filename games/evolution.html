Evolution

Overview

About Evolution

It's a Unity WebGL title, made by Keiwan Donyagard. Somewhere around 30M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. Evolution is a physics-based strategy games pick for anyone who'd rather plan two steps ahead than button-mash.

How to Play Evolution

We don't have a confirmed control list for Evolution. Most browser games in this category respond to arrow keys or WASD for movement and the mouse or space bar for the main action, so start there.

Tips for Evolution

  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.

Why Play Evolution Here

Evolution just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.
