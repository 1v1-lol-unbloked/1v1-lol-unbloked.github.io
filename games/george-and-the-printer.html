George and the Printer

Overview

About George and the Printer

Credit for this one goes to Robin Six — it's built on Unity WebGL. It's sitting at about 500K+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. George and the Printer rewards patience over speed, which is pretty typical for escaping games.

How to Play George and the Printer

George and the Printer's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for George and the Printer

  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.

Why Play George and the Printer Here

You click play and George and the Printer starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

George and the Printer FAQ
