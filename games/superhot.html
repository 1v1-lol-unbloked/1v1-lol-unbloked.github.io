About Superhot

It's a Unity WebGL title, made by SUPERHOT Team. It's picked up around 10M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. Superhot keeps things simple on paper: it's shooter games, and it rewards whoever reacts first.

How to Play Superhot

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Superhot yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Superhot

  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
