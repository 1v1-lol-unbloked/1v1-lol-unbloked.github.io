About Madalin Stunt Cars 2

Under the hood it's Unity WebGL, put together by Madalin Stanchu. Madalin Stunt Cars 2 is a car simulation games game where the difference between a clean run and a wipeout is usually one late input. It's sitting at about 50M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog.
