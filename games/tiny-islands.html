About Tiny Islands

Tiny Islands is a puzzle games pick for anyone who'd rather plan two steps ahead than button-mash. Credit for this one goes to David King — it's built on Unity WebGL. It's sitting at about 5M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog.

How to Play Tiny Islands

We don't have a confirmed control list for Tiny Islands. Most browser games in this category respond to arrow keys or WASD for movement and the mouse or space bar for the main action, so start there.

Tips for Tiny Islands

  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
