About Tiny Islands

Tiny Islands is a puzzle games pick for anyone who'd rather plan two steps ahead than button-mash. Credit for this one goes to David King — it's built on Unity WebGL. It's sitting at about 5M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog.
