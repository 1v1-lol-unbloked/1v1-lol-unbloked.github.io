About Impossible Quiz

Credit for this one goes to Slapp-Me-Do — it's built on Flash. Impossible Quiz rewards patience over speed, which is pretty typical for time waster games. Somewhere around 50M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Impossible Quiz

We don't have a confirmed control list for Impossible Quiz. Most browser games in this category respond to arrow keys or WASD for movement and the mouse or space bar for the main action, so start there.

Tips for Impossible Quiz

  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.

Why Play Impossible Quiz Here

Impossible Quiz FAQ
