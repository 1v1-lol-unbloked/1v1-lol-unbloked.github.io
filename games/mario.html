Mario

Overview

About Mario

Roughly 100M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly. Nintendo built it, running on HTML5, JavaScript. Mario is built around quick reflexes — file it under platformer games and expect very little downtime between decisions.

How to Play Mario

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Mario yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Mario

  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.

Why Play Mario Here

You click play and Mario starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Mario FAQ

Can I play Mario in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Do I need to install anything for Mario?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
