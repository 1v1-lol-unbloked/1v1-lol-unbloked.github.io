Overview

About Gimme the Airpod

Gimme the Airpod is built around quick reflexes — file it under dueling games and expect very little downtime between decisions. Somewhere around 1M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. It's built on Unity WebGL.

How to Play Gimme the Airpod

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Gimme the Airpod yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Gimme the Airpod

  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.

Why Play Gimme the Airpod Here

You click play and Gimme the Airpod starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.
