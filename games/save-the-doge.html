Save the Doge

Overview

About Save the Doge

Save the Doge is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — puzzle games, in case the name didn't give it away. It's picked up around 100M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. Under the hood it's HTML5, JavaScript, put together by Wonder Group.

How to Play Save the Doge

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Save the Doge yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Save the Doge

  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.

Why Play Save the Doge Here

Nothing extra bolted on — just Save the Doge, ready to go.
