The Battle

Overview

About The Battle

Somewhere around 500K+ plays so far, for what that's worth. The Battle is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — tower defense games, in case the name didn't give it away. Free World Group built it, running on Flash.

How to Play The Battle

The Battle's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for The Battle

  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.

Why Play The Battle Here

The Battle just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.

The Battle FAQ

Can I play The Battle in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Why does The Battle sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
