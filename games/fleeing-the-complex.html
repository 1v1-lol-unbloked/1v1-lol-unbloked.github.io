About Fleeing the Complex

It's picked up around 50M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. It's a Flash title, made by PuffballsUnited. Fleeing the Complex is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — escaping games, in case the name didn't give it away.

Tips for Fleeing the Complex
