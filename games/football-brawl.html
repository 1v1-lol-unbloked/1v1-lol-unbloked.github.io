About Football Brawl

It's sitting at about 1M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. It's a HTML5, JavaScript title, made by InLogic Games. Football Brawl belongs in the soccer games category, and it punishes overcorrecting more than it punishes going slow.

How to Play Football Brawl

Football Brawl's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Football Brawl

  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.
  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.
  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.
