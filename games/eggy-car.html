Eggy Car

Overview

About Eggy Car

In this fun and highly skilled car driving game, you have only one goal. Don't let the egg drop to the ground! Manuever the car around the hills and valleys cleverly to keep the egg in your car's container and try not to let it drop and crack! Stop or go forward at just the right time to keep your egg balanced. Collect coins on your ride to buy new cars and try out new strategies! Have fun!

How to Play Eggy Car

Once it loads, Eggy Car responds to:

  • Left Arrow / A / Left Pedal: Backward
  • Right Arrow / D / Right Pedal: Forward

Tips for Eggy Car

  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.
  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.
  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.

Why Play Eggy Car Here

You click play and Eggy Car starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Eggy Car FAQ

Can I play Eggy Car in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for Eggy Car?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
