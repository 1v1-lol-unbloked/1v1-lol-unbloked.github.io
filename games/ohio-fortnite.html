Ohio Fortnite

Overview

About Ohio Fortnite

Ohio Fortnite is built around quick reflexes — file it under shooter games and expect very little downtime between decisions. Under the hood it's Unity WebGL, put together by DiaoChangNan. Somewhere around 5M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Ohio Fortnite

The control scheme for Ohio Fortnite:

  • WASD Keys: Move
  • Mouse: Aim
  • Click: Shoot
  • Space: Jump
  • Shift: Run
  • R: Reload
  • F: Action
  • M: Map
  • Number Keys: Switch Weapons
  • Esc: Pause

Tips for Ohio Fortnite

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.

Why Play Ohio Fortnite Here

You click play and Ohio Fortnite starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Ohio Fortnite FAQ

Can I play Ohio Fortnite in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Do I need to install anything for Ohio Fortnite?

No. It's built with Unity WebGL, so it just runs once the page loads.
