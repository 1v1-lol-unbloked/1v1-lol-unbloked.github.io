About Pixel Smash Duel

Pixel Smash Duel belongs to the dueling category, and it punishes overcorrecting more than it punishes going slow. Somewhere around 1M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. It's a HTML5, JavaScript title, made by RHM Interactive.
