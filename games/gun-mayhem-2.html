About Gun Mayhem 2

Credit for this one goes to Kevin Gu — it's built on Flash. It's picked up around 5M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. Gun Mayhem 2 isn't a game you ease into — it's dueling games, and it wants fast hands and faster decisions.

How to Play Gun Mayhem 2

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Gun Mayhem 2 yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Gun Mayhem 2

  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.

Why does Gun Mayhem 2 sometimes load slow or act a little off?

Yes, on the same keyboard — look at the controls above, there's usually a separate key set listed for each player.
