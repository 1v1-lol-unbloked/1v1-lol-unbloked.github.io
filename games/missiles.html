Overview

About Missiles

Missiles is built around quick reflexes — file it under reaction games and expect very little downtime between decisions. It's picked up around 1M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. Under the hood, it's running HTML5, JavaScript.

How to Play Missiles

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Missiles yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Missiles

  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.

Why Play Missiles Here

Missiles just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.
