Cut the Rope Holiday

Overview

About Cut the Rope Holiday

Under the hood it's HTML5, JavaScript, put together by ZeptoLab. Cut the Rope Holiday is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — physics-based strategy games, in case the name didn't give it away.

How to Play Cut the Rope Holiday

Nothing complicated here — this is what Cut the Rope Holiday uses:

  • Drag: Cut
  • Click: Action

Tips for Cut the Rope Holiday

  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.

Why Play Cut the Rope Holiday Here

Cut the Rope Holiday just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.
