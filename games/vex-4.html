Vex 4

Overview

About Vex 4

Vex 4 is the fourth game in the exciting Vex platformer series. Vex is a very interesting and unique platformer game where you control the player to do all kinds of movements and stunts to move forward, dodge obstacles, collect coins, and reach the end! Do your best to move forward and use checkpoints to save your progress! There's many levels to choose from and enjoy. Have fun!

How to Play Vex 4

Nothing complicated here — this is what Vex 4 uses:

  • Up Arrow: Jump
  • Down Arrow: Slide / Dodge
  • Left / Right Arrows: Move Left / Right
  • Note: WASD keys also work

Tips for Vex 4

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
