2048

Overview

About 2048

2048 is a very popular and classic puzzle game. The goal is to merge the tiles with the same numbers until they merge all the way to 2048. Use arrow keys to move all the tiles. If no tiles can be moved anymore, you fail.

How to Play 2048

2048 keeps its controls short:

  • Arrow / WASD Keys: Move Number Tiles

Tips for 2048

  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.

Why Play 2048 Here
