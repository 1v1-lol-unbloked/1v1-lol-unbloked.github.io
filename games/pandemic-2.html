Pandemic 2

Overview

About Pandemic 2

Pandemic 2 is a puzzle games pick for anyone who'd rather plan two steps ahead than button-mash. Dark Realm Studios built it, running on Flash. It's picked up around 10M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it.

How to Play Pandemic 2

Pandemic 2's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Pandemic 2

  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.

Why Play Pandemic 2 Here

You click play and Pandemic 2 starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Pandemic 2 FAQ

Can I play Pandemic 2 in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Why does Pandemic 2 sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
