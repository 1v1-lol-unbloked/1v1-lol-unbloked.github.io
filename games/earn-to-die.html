Earn to Die

Overview

About Earn to Die

It's sitting at about 100M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. Under the hood it's Unity WebGL, put together by Toffee Games. Earn to Die belongs in the car racing games category, and it punishes overcorrecting more than it punishes going slow.

How to Play Earn to Die

The control scheme for Earn to Die:

  • Up Arrow: Forward
  • Down Arrow: Backward
  • Left / Right Arrows: Tilt
  • Esc: Pause
  • Note: WASD keys also work

Tips for Earn to Die

  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.
  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.
  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.

Why Play Earn to Die Here

Earn to Die just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.

Earn to Die FAQ

Can I play Earn to Die in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.
