About Tag

Under the hood it's HTML5, JavaScript, put together by JetGames. Tag keeps things simple on paper: it's platformer games, and it rewards whoever reacts first. Roughly 10M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly.

How to Play Tag

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Tag yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Tag

  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.
  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.

Why Play Tag Here
