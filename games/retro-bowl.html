Overview

About Retro Bowl

Retro Bowl is less about raw speed and more about control — football games, and small mistakes show up fast. It's a HTML5, JavaScript title, made by New Star Games. It's sitting at about 100M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog.

How to Play Retro Bowl

We don't have a confirmed control list for Retro Bowl. Most browser games in this category respond to arrow keys or WASD for movement and the mouse or space bar for the main action, so start there.

Tips for Retro Bowl

  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.
  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.
  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.

Why Play Retro Bowl Here

You click play and Retro Bowl starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Retro Bowl FAQ

Can I play Retro Bowl in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for Retro Bowl?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
