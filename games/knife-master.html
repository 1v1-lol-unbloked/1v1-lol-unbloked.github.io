Knife Master

Overview

About Knife Master

Knife Master is built around quick reflexes — file it under action games and expect very little downtime between decisions. It's a HTML5, JavaScript title, made by Cuga. Somewhere around 500K+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Knife Master

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Knife Master yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Knife Master

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.

Why Play Knife Master Here

You click play and Knife Master starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Knife Master FAQ

Can I play Knife Master in a browser?
