Among Us

Overview

About Among Us

Among Us is a vastly popular multiplayer game where you are either an imposter or a crewmate. In this fanmade version of the original game, you're the imposter and you're facing bot crewmates. Try to kill everybody before they complete the tasks and without being discovered by moving with arrow keys and clicking on different function buttons in the bottom right corner. Use the best strategy and be sneaky to beat the game and win!

How to Play Among Us

We don't have a confirmed control list for Among Us. Most browser games in this category respond to arrow keys or WASD for movement and the mouse or space bar for the main action, so start there.

Tips for Among Us

  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.

Why Play Among Us Here

Among Us just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.
