My Rusty Submarine

Overview

About My Rusty Submarine

My Rusty Submarine rewards patience over speed, which is pretty typical for teamwork games. Credit for this one goes to Drludwig — it's built on Unity WebGL. It's sitting at about 1M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog.

How to Play My Rusty Submarine

My Rusty Submarine's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for My Rusty Submarine

  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.

Why Play My Rusty Submarine Here
