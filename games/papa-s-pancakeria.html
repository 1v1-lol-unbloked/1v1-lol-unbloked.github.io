About Papa's Pancakeria

Papa's Pancakeria is a management games title built for killing time, not for grinding through a tutorial first. It's sitting at about 30M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. Under the hood it's Flash, put together by Flipline Studios.

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Papa's Pancakeria yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.
