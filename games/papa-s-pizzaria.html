About Papa's Pizzaria

Papa's Pizzaria is a management games title built for killing time, not for grinding through a tutorial first. Credit for this one goes to Flipline Studios — it's built on Flash. Somewhere around 30M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Papa's Pizzaria

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Papa's Pizzaria yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Papa's Pizzaria

  • No need to marathon it — these are built for five-minute chunks, not one long sitting.
  • Checking back occasionally tends to matter more than playing nonstop, especially with idle mechanics.
  • Progress usually saves on its own, so stopping mid-run isn't a big deal.

Why Play Papa's Pizzaria Here

Papa's Pizzaria FAQ

Why does Papa's Pizzaria sometimes load slow or act a little off?
