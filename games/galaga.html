About Galaga

Galaga is built around quick reflexes — file it under arcade games and expect very little downtime between decisions. It's picked up around 1M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. Under the hood it's Flash, put together by Robotube Studios.

How to Play Galaga

We don't have a confirmed control list for Galaga. Most browser games in this category respond to arrow keys or WASD for movement and the mouse or space bar for the main action, so start there.

Tips for Galaga

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.

Galaga FAQ

Can I play Galaga in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Why does Galaga sometimes load slow or act a little off?
