Clicker Heroes

Overview

About Clicker Heroes

In this unique monster themed clicker game, you need to click on a monster in different levels to earn gold, and use that gold to hire new heroes that either upgrade your damage per click or damage per second. You can just idle the game and earn a lot of coins with the heroes. Each time you kill 10 monsters in a level, the next level will be unlocked with higher level monsters that are harder to kill, but are also worth more gold. Attack, kill, and upgrade to defeat the forest of monsters!

How to Play Clicker Heroes

The control scheme for Clicker Heroes:

  • Click: Attack

Tips for Clicker Heroes

  • No need to marathon it — these are built for five-minute chunks, not one long sitting.
  • Progress usually saves on its own, so stopping mid-run isn't a big deal.
  • Checking back occasionally tends to matter more than playing nonstop, especially with idle mechanics.

Why Play Clicker Heroes Here

Clicker Heroes just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.
