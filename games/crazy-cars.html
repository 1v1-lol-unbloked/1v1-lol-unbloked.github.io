Overview

About Crazy Cars

It's picked up around 30M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. Crazy Cars is less about raw speed and more about control — car simulation games, and small mistakes show up fast. Under the hood it's Unity WebGL, put together by No Pressure Studios.

How to Play Crazy Cars

We don't have a confirmed control list for Crazy Cars. Most browser games in this category respond to arrow keys or WASD for movement and the mouse or space bar for the main action, so start there.

Tips for Crazy Cars

  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.
  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.
  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.

Why Play Crazy Cars Here

You click play and Crazy Cars starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.
