Dante

Overview

About Dante

Under the hood it's HTML5, JavaScript, put together by Salvatore Previti. Dante leans hard into platformer games, the kind of game where muscle memory matters more than a strategy guide. Somewhere around 5M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Dante

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Dante yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Dante

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.

Why Play Dante Here

Same layout as everything else in the catalog, so if you bounce off Dante, the next one's one click away.

Dante FAQ

Can I play Dante in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Do I need to install anything for Dante?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
