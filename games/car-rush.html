About Car Rush

It's picked up around 1M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. Under the hood, it's running HTML5, JavaScript. Car Rush is a car racing games game where the difference between a clean run and a wipeout is usually one late input.

How to Play Car Rush

Car Rush's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Car Rush

  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.
  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.
  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.
