Stickman Golf

Overview

About Stickman Golf

It's sitting at about 5M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. Credit for this one goes to Noodlecake Games — it's built on HTML5, JavaScript. Stickman Golf is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — physics-based strategy games, in case the name didn't give it away.

How to Play Stickman Golf

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Stickman Golf yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Stickman Golf

  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.

Why Play Stickman Golf Here
