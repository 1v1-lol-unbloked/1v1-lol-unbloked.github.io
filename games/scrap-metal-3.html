About Scrap Metal 3

Scrap Metal 3 belongs to the car simulation category, and it punishes overcorrecting more than it punishes going slow. It's a Unity WebGL title, made by Ciorbyn. It's picked up around 5M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it.
