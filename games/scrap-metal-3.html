Scrap Metal 3

Overview

About Scrap Metal 3

Scrap Metal 3 belongs in the car simulation games category, and it punishes overcorrecting more than it punishes going slow. It's a Unity WebGL title, made by Ciorbyn. It's picked up around 5M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it.

How to Play Scrap Metal 3

Scrap Metal 3's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Scrap Metal 3

  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.
  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.
  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.

Why Play Scrap Metal 3 Here

Nothing extra bolted on — just Scrap Metal 3, ready to go.
