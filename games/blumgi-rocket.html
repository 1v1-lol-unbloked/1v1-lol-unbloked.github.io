Blumgi Rocket

Overview

About Blumgi Rocket

Blumgi Rocket is a popular and satisfying game where you need to control a rocket-equipped car through all kinds of interesting obstacles and reach the end. Use your driving skills to conquer the unique terrains and use your rocket wisely to shoot through the finish line! Hold the space bar to charge it and use the arrow keys to aim the path of the rocket! Have fun!

How to Play Blumgi Rocket

Nothing complicated here — this is what Blumgi Rocket uses:

  • Arrow / WASD Keys: Move
  • Space: Charge Rocket
  • R: Restart

Tips for Blumgi Rocket

  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
