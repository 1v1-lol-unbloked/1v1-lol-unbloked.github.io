Defend the Tank

Overview

About Defend the Tank

Defend the Tank keeps things simple on paper: it's tower defense games, and it rewards whoever reacts first. It's picked up around 1M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. It's built on HTML5, JavaScript.

How to Play Defend the Tank

Defend the Tank's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Defend the Tank

  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.

Why Play Defend the Tank Here

You click play and Defend the Tank starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Defend the Tank FAQ

Can I play Defend the Tank in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for Defend the Tank?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
