About Flappy Bird

Roughly 100M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly. Under the hood it's HTML5, JavaScript, put together by Dong Nguyen, 3kh0. Flappy Bird doesn't ask much of you — it's time waster games, low-stakes, and easy to pick back up wherever you left off.

How to Play Flappy Bird

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Flappy Bird yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Flappy Bird

  • No need to marathon it — these are built for five-minute chunks, not one long sitting.
  • Progress usually saves on its own, so stopping mid-run isn't a big deal.
  • Checking back occasionally tends to matter more than playing nonstop, especially with idle mechanics.

Why Play Flappy Bird Here

Nothing extra bolted on — just Flappy Bird, ready to go.
