About Flappy Bird

Roughly 100M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly. Under the hood it's HTML5, JavaScript, put together by Dong Nguyen, 3kh0. Flappy Bird doesn't ask much of you — it's time waster games, low-stakes, and easy to pick back up wherever you left off.

Gameplay Notes

Flappy Bird gives you one repeated action and very little room for correction. Light, consistent taps make altitude easier to judge than large bursts of input.
