About Meme 2048

Meme 2048 is a number games pick for anyone who'd rather plan two steps ahead than button-mash. It's sitting at about 1M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. Under the hood it's HTML5, JavaScript, put together by Gabriele Cirulli, 3kh0.

How to Play Meme 2048

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Meme 2048 yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Meme 2048
