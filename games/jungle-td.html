About Jungle TD

Jungle TD is a tower defense games pick for anyone who'd rather plan two steps ahead than button-mash. Under the hood it's Unity WebGL, put together by Beedo Games. It's sitting at about 500K+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog.
