About Stick Merge

TinyDobbins built it, running on HTML5, JavaScript. It's picked up around 30M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. Stick Merge leans hard into shooting games, the kind of game where muscle memory matters more than a strategy guide.

How to Play Stick Merge

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Stick Merge yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Stick Merge

  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.
