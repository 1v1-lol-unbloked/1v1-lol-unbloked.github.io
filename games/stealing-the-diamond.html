Stealing The Diamond

Overview

About Stealing The Diamond

Roughly 30M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly. Credit for this one goes to PuffballsUnited — it's built on Flash. Stealing The Diamond is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — escaping games, in case the name didn't give it away.

How to Play Stealing The Diamond

Stealing The Diamond's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Stealing The Diamond

  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.

Why Play Stealing The Diamond Here

Stealing The Diamond just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.

Stealing The Diamond FAQ

Can I play Stealing The Diamond in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Why does Stealing The Diamond sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
