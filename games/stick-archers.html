Stick Archers

Overview

About Stick Archers

Stick Archers is built around quick reflexes — file it under shooting games and expect very little downtime between decisions. It's a HTML5, JavaScript title, made by RHM Interactive. Somewhere around 5M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Stick Archers

Stick Archers's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Stick Archers

  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.

Why Play Stick Archers Here

Same layout as everything else in the catalog, so if you bounce off Stick Archers, the next one's one click away.

Stick Archers FAQ

Can I play Stick Archers in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Do I need to install anything for Stick Archers?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
