Om Nom Bounce

Overview

About Om Nom Bounce

Om Nom Bounce is built around quick reflexes — file it under tower defense games and expect very little downtime between decisions. Under the hood it's HTML5, JavaScript, put together by ZeptoLab. Somewhere around 1M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Om Nom Bounce

Om Nom Bounce's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Om Nom Bounce

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.

Why Play Om Nom Bounce Here

Nothing extra bolted on — just Om Nom Bounce, ready to go.
