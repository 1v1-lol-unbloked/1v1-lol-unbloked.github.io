Apple Shooter

Overview

About Apple Shooter

Apple Shooter is a simple but addicting game where your goal is to shoot the apple on your friend's head without hitting them! Everytime you hit the apple, you shoot from a little further away and the game becomes more challenging. Can you reach the last level without shooting your friend? The longer you hold, the stronger the arrow!

How to Play Apple Shooter

Apple Shooter keeps its controls short:

  • Drag: Aim
  • Release: shoot

Tips for Apple Shooter

  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.

Why Play Apple Shooter Here

Nothing extra bolted on — just Apple Shooter, ready to go.
