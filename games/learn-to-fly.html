Overview

About Learn To Fly

It's sitting at about 30M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. Credit for this one goes to Light Bringer Games — it's built on Flash. Learn To Fly is the kind of progression games game you open for five minutes and somehow still have open an hour later.

How to Play Learn To Fly

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Learn To Fly yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Learn To Fly

  • Progress usually saves on its own, so stopping mid-run isn't a big deal.
  • No need to marathon it — these are built for five-minute chunks, not one long sitting.
  • Checking back occasionally tends to matter more than playing nonstop, especially with idle mechanics.

Why Play Learn To Fly Here

Nothing extra bolted on — just Learn To Fly, ready to go.

Learn To Fly FAQ

Can I play Learn To Fly in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Why does Learn To Fly sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
