About Baldi's Basics

Under the hood it's Unity WebGL, put together by Micah McGonigal. It's sitting at about 10M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. Baldi's Basics is a horror games title built for killing time, not for grinding through a tutorial first.

How to Play Baldi's Basics

Baldi's Basics's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Baldi's Basics

  • Checking back occasionally tends to matter more than playing nonstop, especially with idle mechanics.
  • Progress usually saves on its own, so stopping mid-run isn't a big deal.
  • No need to marathon it — these are built for five-minute chunks, not one long sitting.

Why Play Baldi's Basics Here
