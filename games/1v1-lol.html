1v1.LOL

Overview

About 1v1.LOL

1v1.LOL is a fun battle royale and survival shooting game where your only goal is to survive. Use the controls smart and well to attack or defense by shooting weapons or building stuff from your materials! React fast and defeat all enemies to win! There are different modes that you can play, with the classic multiplayer battle royale, training obstacles, and more fun maps for you to explore!

How to Play 1v1.LOL

Nothing complicated here — this is what 1v1.LOL uses:

  • WASD Keys: Move
  • Space: Jump
  • Mouse: Turn
  • Left Click: shoot / Use Material
  • Right click: aim
  • Numbers / Certain Letters: Switch Weapons / Materials
  • Esc: Pause

Tips for 1v1.LOL

  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.

Why Play 1v1.LOL Here

You click play and 1v1.LOL starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

1v1.LOL FAQ

Can I play 1v1.LOL in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for 1v1.LOL?

No. It's built with Unity WebGL, so it just runs once the page loads.
