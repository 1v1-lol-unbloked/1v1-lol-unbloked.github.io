About Escaping the Prison

Escaping the Prison is the second game in the popular animated game series called Henry Stickmin. In this game, there are many different options to choose from to escape from the prison! Help Henry choose the right option to escape from the jail heroically, while not getting caught or killed! There are 3 different successful endings to this game and 18 different failures, see if you can find them all!
