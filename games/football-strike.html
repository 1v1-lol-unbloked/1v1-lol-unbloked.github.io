Football Strike

Overview

About Football Strike

Football Strike belongs in the soccer games category, and it punishes overcorrecting more than it punishes going slow. Under the hood, it's running HTML5, JavaScript. Somewhere around 500K+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Football Strike

Football Strike's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Football Strike

  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.
  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.
  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.

Why Play Football Strike Here

You click play and Football Strike starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Football Strike FAQ

Can I play Football Strike in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for Football Strike?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
