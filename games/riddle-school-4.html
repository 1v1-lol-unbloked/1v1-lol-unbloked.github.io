Riddle School 4

Overview

About Riddle School 4

JonBro built it, running on Flash. It's picked up around 30M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. Riddle School 4 is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — escaping games, in case the name didn't give it away.

How to Play Riddle School 4

Riddle School 4's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Riddle School 4

  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.

Riddle School 4 FAQ

Can I play Riddle School 4 in a browser?

Why does Riddle School 4 sometimes load slow or act a little off?
