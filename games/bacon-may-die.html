Bacon May Die

Overview

About Bacon May Die

Bacon May Die keeps things simple on paper: it's run and gun games, and it rewards whoever reacts first. Credit for this one goes to SnoutUp Games — it's built on HTML5, JavaScript. Somewhere around 50M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Bacon May Die

Bacon May Die's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Bacon May Die

  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.

Why Play Bacon May Die Here

You click play and Bacon May Die starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Can I play Bacon May Die in a browser?
