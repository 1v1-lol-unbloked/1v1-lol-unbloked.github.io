About G-Switch

G-Switch is built around quick reflexes — file it under reaction games and expect very little downtime between decisions. It's sitting at about 50M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. Credit for this one goes to Serius Games — it's built on HTML5, JavaScript.

How to Play G-Switch

G-Switch keeps its controls short:

  • Any Key: Flip Gravity
  • Esc: Pause

Tips for G-Switch

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.

Why Play G-Switch Here

Nothing extra bolted on — just G-Switch, ready to go.
