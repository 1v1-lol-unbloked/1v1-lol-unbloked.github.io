About Snail Bob 8

Snail Bob 8 is a point and click strategy games pick for anyone who'd rather plan two steps ahead than button-mash. Under the hood it's HTML5, JavaScript, put together by Hunter Hamster Studio. It's picked up around 5M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it.

How to Play Snail Bob 8

Snail Bob 8's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Snail Bob 8

  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
