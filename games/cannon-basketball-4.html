About Cannon Basketball 4

Cannon Basketball 4 is a physics-based strategy games pick for anyone who'd rather plan two steps ahead than button-mash. Somewhere around 5M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. Credit for this one goes to Oleg Kuzyk — it's built on HTML5, JavaScript.

How to Play Cannon Basketball 4

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Cannon Basketball 4 yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Cannon Basketball 4

  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
