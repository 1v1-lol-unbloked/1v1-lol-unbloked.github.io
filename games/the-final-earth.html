The Final Earth

Overview

About The Final Earth

The Final Earth is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — management games, in case the name didn't give it away. Credit for this one goes to Florian van Strien — it's built on HTML5, JavaScript. Somewhere around 5M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play The Final Earth

The Final Earth's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for The Final Earth

  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.

Why Play The Final Earth Here

Same layout as everything else in the catalog, so if you bounce off The Final Earth, the next one's one click away.

The Final Earth FAQ

Can I play The Final Earth in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
