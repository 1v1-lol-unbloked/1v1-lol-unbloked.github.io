OvO

Overview

About OvO

Credit for this one goes to Dedra Games — it's built on HTML5, JavaScript. Roughly 100M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly. OvO keeps things simple on paper: it's platformer games, and it rewards whoever reacts first.

How to Play OvO

OvO's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for OvO

  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.

Why Play OvO Here

Nothing extra bolted on — just OvO, ready to go.

OvO FAQ

Can I play OvO in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for OvO?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
