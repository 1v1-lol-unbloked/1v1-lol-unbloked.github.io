Bomb It 7

Overview

About Bomb It 7

It's a HTML5, JavaScript title, made by Spil Games. Bomb It 7 keeps things simple on paper: it's arcade games, and it rewards whoever reacts first. Somewhere around 10M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Bomb It 7

Bomb It 7 keeps its controls short:

  • Arrow Keys: Move (Player 1)
  • Space: Drop Bomb (Player 1)
  • WASD Keys: Move (Player 2)
  • Enter: Drop Bomb (Player 2)

Tips for Bomb It 7

  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.

Why Play Bomb It 7 Here

You click play and Bomb It 7 starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Bomb It 7 FAQ

Can I play Bomb It 7 in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for Bomb It 7?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.

Can two people play Bomb It 7 at once?

Yes, on the same keyboard — look at the controls above, there's usually a separate key set listed for each player.
