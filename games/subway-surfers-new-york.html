About Subway Surfers New York

Subway Surfers, a fun and addicting endless parkour game, is one of the most iconic and well-known mobile games in the world! Your mission is to escape from the police, collect coins, while dodging the obstacles! Complete mission sets to boost your score and use upgrades wisely! Use hoverboard to save you from crashing and keys to revive. You can also get power ups that upgrade you for a certain amount of time. Use the coins to purchase characters, upgrades, hoverboards, and other stuff! In this interesting New York themed playground, see how far you can go!
