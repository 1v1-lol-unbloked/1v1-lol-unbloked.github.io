Skywire

Overview

About Skywire

Nitrome built it, running on Flash. Skywire isn't a game you ease into — it's timing games, and it wants fast hands and faster decisions. It's sitting at about 500K+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog.

How to Play Skywire

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Skywire yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Skywire

  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.
  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.

Why Play Skywire Here

Same layout as everything else in the catalog, so if you bounce off Skywire, the next one's one click away.

Skywire FAQ

Can I play Skywire in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Why does Skywire sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
