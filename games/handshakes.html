About Handshakes

Handshakes is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — puzzle games, in case the name didn't give it away. It's sitting at about 1M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. Under the hood it's Unity WebGL, put together by Pet Pumpkin.

How to Play Handshakes

We don't have a confirmed control list for Handshakes. Most browser games in this category respond to arrow keys or WASD for movement and the mouse or space bar for the main action, so start there.

Tips for Handshakes

  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.

Why Play Handshakes Here
