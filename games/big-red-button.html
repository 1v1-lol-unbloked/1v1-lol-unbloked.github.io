About Big Red Button

Big Red Button keeps things breezy. It's casual games, nothing to memorize, nothing to lose sleep over. Under the hood, it's running Flash. It's picked up around 1M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it.

How to Play Big Red Button

Big Red Button's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Big Red Button

  • No need to marathon it — these are built for five-minute chunks, not one long sitting.

Big Red Button FAQ

Can I play Big Red Button in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Why does Big Red Button sometimes load slow or act a little off?
