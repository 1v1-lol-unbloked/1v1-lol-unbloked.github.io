About Pizza Tower

It's sitting at about 50M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. It's a HTML5, JavaScript title, made by Tour De Pizza. Pizza Tower is built around quick reflexes — file it under platformer games and expect very little downtime between decisions.

How to Play Pizza Tower

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Pizza Tower yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Pizza Tower

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
