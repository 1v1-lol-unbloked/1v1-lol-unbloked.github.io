Bloons TD 2

Overview

About Bloons TD 2

Bloons TD 2 slows things down on purpose. It's tower defense games, and rushing usually backfires. Ninja Kiwi built it, running on Flash. Roughly 30M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly.

How to Play Bloons TD 2

Bloons TD 2's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Bloons TD 2

  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.

Why Play Bloons TD 2 Here

You click play and Bloons TD 2 starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Bloons TD 2 FAQ

Can I play Bloons TD 2 in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Why does Bloons TD 2 sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
