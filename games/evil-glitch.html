About Evil Glitch

Evil Glitch is built around quick reflexes — file it under shooting games and expect very little downtime between decisions. Somewhere around 5M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. Credit for this one goes to Agar3s — it's built on HTML5, JavaScript.

How to Play Evil Glitch

Evil Glitch's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Evil Glitch

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.

Why Play Evil Glitch Here

Same layout as everything else in the catalog, so if you bounce off Evil Glitch, the next one's one click away.
