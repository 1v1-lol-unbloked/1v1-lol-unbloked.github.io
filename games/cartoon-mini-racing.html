About Cartoon Mini Racing

It's sitting at about 10M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. Cartoon Mini Racing puts you behind the wheel in a racing games setup where timing beats flooring it. Credit for this one goes to Brain Software — it's built on Unity WebGL.

How to Play Cartoon Mini Racing

Once it loads, Cartoon Mini Racing responds to:

  • Arrow Keys: Drive
  • H: Reset Car

Tips for Cartoon Mini Racing

  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.
  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.
  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.

Why Play Cartoon Mini Racing Here
