About Bad Ice Cream 3

Bad Ice Cream 3 rewards patience over speed, which is pretty typical for puzzle games. Under the hood it's Flash, put together by Nitrome.

How to Play Bad Ice Cream 3

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Bad Ice Cream 3 yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Bad Ice Cream 3

  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
