Glitch Dash

Overview

About Glitch Dash

David Marquardt built it, running on Unity WebGL. Somewhere around 10M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. Glitch Dash leans hard into endless runner games, the kind of game where muscle memory matters more than a strategy guide.

How to Play Glitch Dash

Nothing complicated here — this is what Glitch Dash uses:

  • Arrow Keys: Move
  • S: Use Shield

Tips for Glitch Dash

  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.

Why Play Glitch Dash Here

Glitch Dash just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.

Glitch Dash FAQ

Can I play Glitch Dash in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Do I need to install anything for Glitch Dash?

No. It's built with Unity WebGL, so it just runs once the page loads.
