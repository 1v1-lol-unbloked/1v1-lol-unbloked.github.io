Roughly 1M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly. CraftMine drops you straight into adventure games territory, where hesitating usually costs you the round. Under the hood, it's running HTML5, JavaScript.

How to Play CraftMine

Nobody's logged the exact controls for CraftMine yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for CraftMine

  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
