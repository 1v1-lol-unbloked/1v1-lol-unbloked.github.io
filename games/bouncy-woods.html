About Bouncy Woods

Credit for this one goes to Robowhale — it's built on HTML5, JavaScript. It's picked up around 5M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. Bouncy Woods is a physics-based strategy games pick for anyone who'd rather plan two steps ahead than button-mash.

How to Play Bouncy Woods

Once it loads, Bouncy Woods responds to:

  • Drag: Aim
  • Release: Shoot

Tips for Bouncy Woods

  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
