About Monster Truck Destroyer

Somewhere around 500K+ plays so far, for what that's worth. Monster Truck Destroyer belongs in the car racing games category, and it punishes overcorrecting more than it punishes going slow. Under the hood it's HTML5, JavaScript, put together by Gametornado.

How to Play Monster Truck Destroyer

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Monster Truck Destroyer yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Monster Truck Destroyer

  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.
  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.
  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.
