About Papa's Freezeria

Flipline Studios built it, running on Flash. Roughly 30M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly. Papa's Freezeria is a management games title built for killing time, not for grinding through a tutorial first.

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Papa's Freezeria yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.
