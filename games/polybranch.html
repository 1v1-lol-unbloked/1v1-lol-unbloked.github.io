About PolyBranch

PolyBranch keeps things simple on paper: it's reaction games, and it rewards whoever reacts first. Somewhere around 5M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. gbatha built it, running on HTML5, JavaScript.

How to Play PolyBranch

We don't have a confirmed control list for PolyBranch. Most browser games in this category respond to arrow keys or WASD for movement and the mouse or space bar for the main action, so start there.

Tips for PolyBranch

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.

Why Play PolyBranch Here
