Hanger

Overview

About Hanger

Under the hood it's Flash, put together by A Small Game. Hanger slows things down on purpose. It's physics-based strategy games, and rushing usually backfires. It's picked up around 1M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it.

How to Play Hanger

Once it loads, Hanger responds to:

  • Hold: Hang
  • Release: Detach

Tips for Hanger

  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.

Why Play Hanger Here

Same layout as everything else in the catalog, so if you bounce off Hanger, the next one's one click away.

Hanger FAQ

Can I play Hanger in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Why does Hanger sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
