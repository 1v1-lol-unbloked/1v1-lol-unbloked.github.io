Ninja Cat

Overview

About Ninja Cat

Roughly 1M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly. It's built on HTML5, JavaScript. Ninja Cat is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — physics-based strategy games, in case the name didn't give it away.

How to Play Ninja Cat

The control scheme for Ninja Cat:

  • Hold: Aim
  • Release: Jump

Tips for Ninja Cat

  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.

Why Play Ninja Cat Here

You click play and Ninja Cat starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.
