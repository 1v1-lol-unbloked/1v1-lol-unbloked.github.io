Bloxors

Overview

About Bloxors

Somewhere around 50M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. Credit for this one goes to Damien Clarke — it's built on Flash. Bloxors is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — puzzle games, in case the name didn't give it away.

How to Play Bloxors

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Bloxors yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Bloxors

  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.

Why Play Bloxors Here

Bloxors just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.

Bloxors FAQ

Can I play Bloxors in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Why does Bloxors sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
