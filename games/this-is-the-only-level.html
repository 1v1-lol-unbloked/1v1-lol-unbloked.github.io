About This Is The Only Level

It's sitting at about 10M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. Under the hood it's Flash, put together by jmtb02 (John Cooney). This Is The Only Level keeps things simple on paper: it's platformer games, and it rewards whoever reacts first.

How to Play This Is The Only Level

Nobody's logged the exact controls for This Is The Only Level yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for This Is The Only Level

  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
