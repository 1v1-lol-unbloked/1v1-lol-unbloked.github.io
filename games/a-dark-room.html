A Dark Room

Overview

About A Dark Room

A Dark Room is a fun game where you can make decisions on what to do, what to make, or what materials to gather in order to survive and beat the game. Simply click on the buttons to do different things and build a village.

How to Play A Dark Room

Once it loads, A Dark Room responds to:

  • Click: Action

Tips for A Dark Room

  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.

Why Play A Dark Room Here

A Dark Room just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.

A Dark Room FAQ

Can I play A Dark Room in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for A Dark Room?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
