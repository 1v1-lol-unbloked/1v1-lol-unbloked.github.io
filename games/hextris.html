About Hextris

Roughly 30M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly. Hextris isn't a game you ease into — it's reaction games, and it wants fast hands and faster decisions. Under the hood it's HTML5, JavaScript, put together by Logan Engstrom.

How to Play Hextris

We don't have a confirmed control list for Hextris. Most browser games in this category respond to arrow keys or WASD for movement and the mouse or space bar for the main action, so start there.

Tips for Hextris

  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.

Why Play Hextris Here

You click play and Hextris starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.
