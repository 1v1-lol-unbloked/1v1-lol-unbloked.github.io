Hex Empire

Overview

About Hex Empire

Under the hood it's Flash, put together by Minijuegos. It's sitting at about 500K+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. Hex Empire slows things down on purpose. It's puzzle games, and rushing usually backfires.

How to Play Hex Empire

Hex Empire's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Hex Empire

  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.

Why Play Hex Empire Here

Same layout as everything else in the catalog, so if you bounce off Hex Empire, the next one's one click away.

Hex Empire FAQ

Can I play Hex Empire in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Why does Hex Empire sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
