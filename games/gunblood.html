Gunblood

Overview

About Gunblood

Somewhere around 5M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. Wolf Games built it, running on Flash. Gunblood keeps things simple on paper: it's shooting games, and it rewards whoever reacts first.

How to Play Gunblood

Gunblood's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Gunblood

  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.

Why Play Gunblood Here

Same layout as everything else in the catalog, so if you bounce off Gunblood, the next one's one click away.

Gunblood FAQ

Can I play Gunblood in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Why does Gunblood sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
