Wheelie Bike

Overview

About Wheelie Bike

Somewhere around 1M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. Wheelie Bike belongs in the biking games category, and it punishes overcorrecting more than it punishes going slow. It's built on HTML5, JavaScript.

How to Play Wheelie Bike

Wheelie Bike's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Wheelie Bike

  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.
  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.
  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.

Why Play Wheelie Bike Here

You click play and Wheelie Bike starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Wheelie Bike FAQ

Can I play Wheelie Bike in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Do I need to install anything for Wheelie Bike?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
