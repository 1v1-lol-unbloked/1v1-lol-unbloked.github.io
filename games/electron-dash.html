Electron Dash

Overview

About Electron Dash

It's a Unity WebGL title, made by DQV Studio. Electron Dash keeps things simple on paper: it's endless runner games, and it rewards whoever reacts first. Somewhere around 1M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Electron Dash

Once it loads, Electron Dash responds to:

  • Left / Right Arrows: Dash Left / Right
  • Up Arrow / Space: Jump
  • Note: WASD keys also work

Tips for Electron Dash

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.

Why Play Electron Dash Here

Electron Dash just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.

Electron Dash FAQ

Can I play Electron Dash in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for Electron Dash?

No. It's built with Unity WebGL, so it just runs once the page loads.
