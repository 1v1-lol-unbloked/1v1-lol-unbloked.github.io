Boxel Rebound

Overview

About Boxel Rebound

Boxel Rebound is a poopular Chrome extension platformer game, brought to the web by MacVG. In this fast-paced reaction game, you need to control a block to jump over obstacles, spikes, and cliffs. Try to reach the end as fast as possible! As you advance, levels will be getting harder and there will also be other techniques like trampolines, speed up blocks, and resizers. Jump at the right time to avoid dying and get as many points as possible! Other than the preloaded 50 levels, you can also create your own unique fun levels and enjoy it yourself or with friends! Have fun!

How to Play Boxel Rebound

Once it loads, Boxel Rebound responds to:

  • Space Bar / Up Arrow / Click: Jump

Tips for Boxel Rebound

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.

Why Play Boxel Rebound Here

Nothing extra bolted on — just Boxel Rebound, ready to go.

Boxel Rebound FAQ

Can I play Boxel Rebound in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Do I need to install anything for Boxel Rebound?
