About Endless Tunnel

Under the hood, it's running Unity WebGL. Endless Tunnel isn't a game you ease into — it's reaction games, and it wants fast hands and faster decisions. Somewhere around 500K+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Endless Tunnel

Endless Tunnel keeps its controls short:

  • Left / Right Arrow Keys: Turn Left / Right

Tips for Endless Tunnel

  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.
  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.

Why Play Endless Tunnel Here

You click play and Endless Tunnel starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Endless Tunnel FAQ

Can I play Endless Tunnel in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Do I need to install anything for Endless Tunnel?

No. It's built with Unity WebGL, so it just runs once the page loads.
