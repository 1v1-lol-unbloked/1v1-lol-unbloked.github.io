Big Shot Boxing

Overview

About Big Shot Boxing

Big Shot Boxing is less about raw speed and more about control — sports arcade games, and small mistakes show up fast. It's picked up around 10M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. Colin Lane Games AB built it, running on HTML5, JavaScript.

How to Play Big Shot Boxing

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Big Shot Boxing yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Big Shot Boxing

  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.
  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.
  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.

Why Play Big Shot Boxing Here

You click play and Big Shot Boxing starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Big Shot Boxing FAQ

Can I play Big Shot Boxing in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for Big Shot Boxing?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
