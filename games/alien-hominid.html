Alien Hominid

Overview

About Alien Hominid

Alien Hominid is a fast-paced action game where you control an alien and shoot or dodge the police officers!

How to Play Alien Hominid

Alien Hominid's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Alien Hominid

  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.

Why Play Alien Hominid Here

Alien Hominid just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.

Alien Hominid FAQ

Can I play Alien Hominid in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Why does Alien Hominid sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
