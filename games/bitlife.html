BitLife

Overview

About BitLife

In BitLife, this exciting life simulation game, you can literally be anyone. Write your own life story from birth to death however you like by choosing different options, do different things, and make different decisions. Click on the green button to age, and as you age, there will be situations where you'll need to make the best decisions that will make your character successful. You can get a dream job, get married, have children... you can do anything. Experience countless lives all in your control in BitLife!

How to Play BitLife

BitLife keeps its controls short:

  • Click: Action

Tips for BitLife

  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.

BitLife FAQ

Can I play BitLife in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Do I need to install anything for BitLife?

No. It's built with Unity WebGL, so it just runs once the page loads.
