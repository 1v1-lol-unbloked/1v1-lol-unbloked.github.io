About Burnin' Rubber 5 XS

Credit for this one goes to XformGames — it's built on Unity WebGL. Somewhere around 10M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. Burnin' Rubber 5 XS puts you behind the wheel in a racing games setup where timing beats flooring it.

How to Play Burnin' Rubber 5 XS

Burnin' Rubber 5 XS keeps its controls short:

  • Arrow Keys: Drive
  • Z: Weapon 1
  • X: Weapon 2

Tips for Burnin' Rubber 5 XS

  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.
  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.
  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.
