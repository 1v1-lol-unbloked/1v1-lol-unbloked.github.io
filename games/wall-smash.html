About Wall Smash

It's built on HTML5, JavaScript. Wall Smash is a physics-based strategy games pick for anyone who'd rather plan two steps ahead than button-mash. Roughly 1M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly.

How to Play Wall Smash

Wall Smash's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Wall Smash

  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.

Why Play Wall Smash Here
