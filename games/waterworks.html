Overview

About Waterworks!

Waterworks! is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — management games, in case the name didn't give it away. Somewhere around 1M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. It's a HTML5, JavaScript title, made by scriptwelder.

How to Play Waterworks!

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Waterworks! yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Waterworks!

  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.

Why Play Waterworks! Here

Same layout as everything else in the catalog, so if you bounce off Waterworks!, the next one's one click away.
