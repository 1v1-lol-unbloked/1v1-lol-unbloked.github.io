Helicopter

Overview

About Helicopter

Under the hood, it's running Flash. Helicopter is built around quick reflexes — file it under reaction games and expect very little downtime between decisions. Somewhere around 1M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Helicopter

Once it loads, Helicopter responds to:

  • Click: Fly Up

Tips for Helicopter

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.

Why Play Helicopter Here

Helicopter just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.

Helicopter FAQ

Can I play Helicopter in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Why does Helicopter sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
