About Toss the Turtle

Toss the Turtle leans hard into progression games, the kind of game where muscle memory matters more than a strategy guide. Credit for this one goes to Foreverkul — it's built on Flash. Roughly 50M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly.
