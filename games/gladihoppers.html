About Gladihoppers

It's a Unity WebGL title, made by Dreamon Studios. It's picked up around 10M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. Gladihoppers drops you straight into dueling games territory, where hesitating usually costs you the round.

How to Play Gladihoppers

The control scheme for Gladihoppers:

  • WASD Keys: Move
  • Arrow Keys: Attack
  • Space: Switch Stance
  • Esc: Pause

Tips for Gladihoppers

  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
