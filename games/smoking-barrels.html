Smoking Barrels

Overview

About Smoking Barrels

Smoking Barrels is built around quick reflexes — file it under shooting games and expect very little downtime between decisions. It's a Flash title, made by GmaeSheep. Somewhere around 5M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Smoking Barrels

Smoking Barrels's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Smoking Barrels

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.

Why Play Smoking Barrels Here

You click play and Smoking Barrels starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Smoking Barrels FAQ

Can I play Smoking Barrels in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Why does Smoking Barrels sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
