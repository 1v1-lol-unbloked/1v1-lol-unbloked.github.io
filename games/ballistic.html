About Ballistic

Ballistic is an amazing version of the classic ball shooting game, where your goal is to shoot the balls just at the right angle to eliminate the blocks on the screen! Each time a ball hits a block, the number decreases by 1. If a block touches the bottom, the game's over. You can also get new balls by aiming for the white dots. You need to be very clever to find the perfect angle to make the balls bounce multiple times thus eliminating more blocks. Good luck!
