House of Hazards

Overview

About House of Hazards

House of Hazards is built around quick reflexes — file it under action games and expect very little downtime between decisions. Credit for this one goes to New Eich Games — it's built on Unity WebGL. Somewhere around 10M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play House of Hazards

House of Hazards's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for House of Hazards

  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.

Why Play House of Hazards Here

Nothing extra bolted on — just House of Hazards, ready to go.

House of Hazards FAQ

Can I play House of Hazards in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for House of Hazards?

No. It's built with Unity WebGL, so it just runs once the page loads.
