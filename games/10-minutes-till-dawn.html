10 Minutes Till Dawn

Overview

About 10 Minutes Till Dawn

10 Minutes Till Dawn is a very exciting fantasy survival game for you to play! Use WASD to move and dodge the creatures or else you'll loose hearts and die. Use your mouse to aim and click to shoot. You can collect the creature's coins when they die and upgrade your character to gain abilities!

How to Play 10 Minutes Till Dawn

Once it loads, 10 Minutes Till Dawn responds to:

  • WASD Keys: Move
  • Click: Shoot
  • R: Reload
  • Esc: Pause

Tips for 10 Minutes Till Dawn

  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.

Why Play 10 Minutes Till Dawn Here

You click play and 10 Minutes Till Dawn starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

10 Minutes Till Dawn FAQ

Can I play 10 Minutes Till Dawn in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for 10 Minutes Till Dawn?

No. It's built with Unity WebGL, so it just runs once the page loads.
