Kingdom Rush

Overview

About Kingdom Rush

Under the hood it's Unity WebGL, put together by Ironhide Games. Kingdom Rush is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — tower defense games, in case the name didn't give it away. Roughly 50M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly.

How to Play Kingdom Rush

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Kingdom Rush yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Kingdom Rush

  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.

Why Play Kingdom Rush Here

You click play and Kingdom Rush starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Kingdom Rush FAQ

Can I play Kingdom Rush in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Do I need to install anything for Kingdom Rush?

No. It's built with Unity WebGL, so it just runs once the page loads.
