About Riddle School 3

Somewhere around 30M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. It's a Flash title, made by JonBro. Riddle School 3 is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — escaping games, in case the name didn't give it away.

How to Play Riddle School 3

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Riddle School 3 yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Riddle School 3
