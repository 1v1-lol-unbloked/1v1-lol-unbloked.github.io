About Cut the Rope

⚠️ Warning: because of unknown issues, the display is broken for this game in normal view, so fullscreen is recommended for this game! Cut the Rope slows things down on purpose. It's physics-based strategy games, and rushing usually backfires. Somewhere around 100M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. Under the hood it's HTML5, JavaScript, put together by ZeptoLab.

How to Play Cut the Rope

Cut the Rope keeps its controls short:

  • Drag: Cut
  • Click: Action

Tips for Cut the Rope

  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
