About Achievement Unlocked

Achievement Unlocked is a really interesting game where you literally play it to unlock 100 achievements. You need to use arrow keys to move your elephant and use your creativity through out the game to unlock all achivements!

How to Play Achievement Unlocked

The control scheme for Achievement Unlocked:

  • Arrow Keys: Move

Tips for Achievement Unlocked

  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.

Why Play Achievement Unlocked Here

Achievement Unlocked FAQ

Can I play Achievement Unlocked in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Why does Achievement Unlocked sometimes load slow or act a little off?
