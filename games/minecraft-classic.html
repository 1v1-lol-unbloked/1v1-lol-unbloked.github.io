About Minecraft Classic

It's sitting at about 500M+ plays, which puts it well past most of the catalog. Mojang Studios built it, running on HTML5, JavaScript. Minecraft Classic isn't a game you ease into — it's adventure games, and it wants fast hands and faster decisions.

Gameplay Notes

Minecraft Classic focuses on the early creative building loop: place blocks, remove them and shape the world without the later survival systems that define modern Minecraft.
