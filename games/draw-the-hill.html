Draw the Hill

Overview

About Draw the Hill

Draw the Hill is less about raw speed and more about control — time waster games, and small mistakes show up fast. It's a HTML5, JavaScript title, made by Stelennnn. It's picked up around 10M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it.

How to Play Draw the Hill

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Draw the Hill yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Draw the Hill

  • Braking a beat before the turn, not during it, keeps you from losing control.
  • Small corrections beat sharp ones almost every time.
  • The first stretch usually plays out the same way each run — learn it and the rest gets easier.

Why Play Draw the Hill Here

You click play and Draw the Hill starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Draw the Hill FAQ

Can I play Draw the Hill in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for Draw the Hill?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
