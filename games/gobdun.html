Gobdun

Overview

About Gobdun

Venturous built it, running on HTML5, JavaScript. Gobdun isn't a game you ease into — it's action games, and it wants fast hands and faster decisions. Somewhere around 5M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Gobdun

Nothing complicated here — this is what Gobdun uses:

  • Up Arrow: Move Forward / Attack
  • Down Arrow: Turn Around / Block
  • Left / Right Arrow Keys: Turn Left / Right
  • P: Pause
  • Note: WASD keys also work

Tips for Gobdun

  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.

Why Play Gobdun Here

Gobdun just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.

Gobdun FAQ

Can I play Gobdun in a browser?

Yes. This page runs the browser version directly.

Do I need to install anything for Gobdun?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
