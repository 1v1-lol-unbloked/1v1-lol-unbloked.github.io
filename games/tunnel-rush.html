Tunnel Rush

Overview

About Tunnel Rush

Roughly 30M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly. Credit for this one goes to Deer Cat Games — it's built on Unity WebGL. Tunnel Rush isn't a game you ease into — it's reaction games, and it wants fast hands and faster decisions.

How to Play Tunnel Rush

Tunnel Rush's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Tunnel Rush

  • If the same spot keeps killing you, don't just retry — change what you're actually doing.
  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.

Why Play Tunnel Rush Here

You click play and Tunnel Rush starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Tunnel Rush FAQ

Can I play Tunnel Rush in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Do I need to install anything for Tunnel Rush?

No. It's built with Unity WebGL, so it just runs once the page loads.
