About Plants vs Zombies

Plants vs Zombies rewards patience over speed, which is pretty typical for tower defense games. Under the hood, it's running Scratch. Roughly 500K+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly.

How to Play Plants vs Zombies

Plants vs Zombies's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Plants vs Zombies

  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
