Overview

About Cubefield

It's picked up around 5M+ plays over time, so plenty of people have already run into it. Cubefield isn't a game you ease into — it's reaction games, and it wants fast hands and faster decisions. Under the hood it's HTML5, JavaScript, put together by Nils Asejevs.

How to Play Cubefield

Nobody's logged the exact controls for Cubefield yet, honestly. Safest bet: fire it up and try arrow keys, WASD, mouse or space — one of those almost always does something.

Tips for Cubefield

  • The moment right before you die is usually where the real timing window was.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.
  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.

Why Play Cubefield Here

Cubefield just runs in the browser here — no installer, no login wall, no fifteen-second unskippable ad before it loads.
