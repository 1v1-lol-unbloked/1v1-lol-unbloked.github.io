Woodventure

Overview

About Woodventure

Woodventure is more of a thinking game than a reflex one — puzzle games, in case the name didn't give it away. Credit for this one goes to Tomas Rychnovsky — it's built on HTML5, JavaScript. Somewhere around 1M+ plays so far, for what that's worth.

How to Play Woodventure

Woodventure's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Woodventure

  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • Every level tends to have one obstacle it's clearly built around — find that first, then work backward.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.

Why Play Woodventure Here

You click play and Woodventure starts. That's really the whole pitch for this page.

Woodventure FAQ

Can I play Woodventure in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Do I need to install anything for Woodventure?

No. It's built with HTML5, JavaScript, so it just runs once the page loads.
