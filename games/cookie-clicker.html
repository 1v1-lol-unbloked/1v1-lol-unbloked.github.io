About Cookie Clicker

Cookie Clicker keeps things breezy. It's clicker games, nothing to memorize, nothing to lose sleep over. Somewhere around 100M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. Credit for this one goes to Orteil — it's built on HTML5, JavaScript.

Gameplay Notes

Cookie Clicker starts with manual clicks but quickly becomes a question of production. Compare what an upgrade changes before buying it; the best purchase is not always the cheapest one currently available.
