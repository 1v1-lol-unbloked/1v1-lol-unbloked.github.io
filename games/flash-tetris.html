About Flash Tetris

Somewhere around 100M+ plays so far, for what that's worth. Flash Tetris is a puzzle games pick for anyone who'd rather plan two steps ahead than button-mash. Nintendo built it, running on Flash.

How to Play Flash Tetris

We don't have a confirmed control list for Flash Tetris. Most browser games in this category respond to arrow keys or WASD for movement and the mouse or space bar for the main action, so start there.

Tips for Flash Tetris

  • If the obvious move isn't working, the answer is probably hiding in whatever mechanic you've been ignoring.
  • There's rarely a clock forcing your hand — take the extra few seconds to think.
  • Undo a mistake early. Letting it snowball is usually worse than starting the move over.

Flash Tetris FAQ

Can I play Flash Tetris in a browser?

Yeah — it loads right from this page, nothing to download.

Why does Flash Tetris sometimes load slow or act a little off?

It's an old Flash game running through an emulation layer these days, so it's not always as smooth as something built natively for the browser. Varies by browser too.
