About Rooftop Snipers

New Eich Games built it, running on Unity WebGL. Roughly 30M+ plays and counting — not a hidden gem exactly. Rooftop Snipers is built around quick reflexes — file it under dueling games and expect very little downtime between decisions.

How to Play Rooftop Snipers

Rooftop Snipers's controls aren't documented on our end. Try the obvious stuff first — WASD, arrows, click, space — the game usually makes it clear once you're moving.

Tips for Rooftop Snipers

  • Enemies and obstacles usually repeat a pattern — watch it once before you commit to a move.
  • Panic makes you slower, not faster. Sounds obvious, matters anyway.
  • Short, deliberate inputs beat holding a button down and hoping.

Why Play Rooftop Snipers Here
