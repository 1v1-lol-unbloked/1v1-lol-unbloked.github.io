About Dinosaurs Merge Master

In this fun idle dino simulation game, your goal is to buy dinosaurus and other attacking units, merge them, upgrade them, and defeat the enemies before they defeat you! Each game you win you'll earn coins that can be used to buy new dinos. Simply buy a dino, and it will attack the enemies automatically! You can also drag the dino to a specific place on the map for a better attacking adventage! Merge your way to victory, and have fun!
