Our story

About Us

1v1 LOL Unblocked is an independent browser game directory designed to make discovering free online games simple. Our catalog brings action, adventure, strategy, puzzle, casual, driving and sports titles together in a fast interface that works on desktop and mobile devices.

What we do

We organize game information, controls, categories and release details so visitors can decide what to play. Popular and new-game pages provide additional ways to browse the catalog, while search and favorites help players return to games they enjoy.

Game availability

Games are loaded from third-party hosts and remain the property of their respective owners and developers. We do not claim ownership of third-party game brands. Availability can change when a provider updates or removes a title.

Our commitment

We aim to maintain a useful, responsive and transparent website. If you find an incorrect description, broken game, rights issue or privacy concern, please use our contact page.
