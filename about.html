About the publisher

About 1v1 LOL Unblocked

1v1 LOL Unblocked is an independent browser-game directory built to help visitors discover, compare and open free web games from one clear catalog. We organize hundreds of titles across action, adventure, strategy, puzzle, casual, driving and sports categories, with dedicated pages that explain what each game is, its basic controls and useful gameplay tips.

What visitors can do here

You can search the catalog, browse popular and newly added games, filter by category, save favorites in your browser and return to recently played titles. Individual game pages are designed to provide useful context before play rather than functioning as bare game embeds.

How our content is prepared

Game titles are reviewed and organized into relevant categories. We write and maintain page descriptions, control summaries, gameplay guidance and navigation around the catalog. We may correct or update a page when a game changes, a link stops working or information becomes inaccurate.

Third-party games and ownership

Some playable games are delivered by third-party hosts. Game names, artwork, trademarks, code and other intellectual property remain the property of their respective owners. Inclusion in our directory does not imply ownership, endorsement or affiliation unless explicitly stated.

Corrections and rights requests

If you find an inaccurate description, broken game, privacy concern or intellectual-property issue, use our Contact page. Copyright owners can also review the information requested on our DMCA Policy.

Our publishing goal

Our goal is a fast, transparent and easy-to-navigate browser-game resource. We continually improve catalog organization, page quality, mobile usability and technical performance so visitors can make an informed choice about what to play.
